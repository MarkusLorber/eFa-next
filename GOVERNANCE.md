# Governance

eFa-next is a private open source project, written and maintained by Markus Lorber.

- The maintainer decides what goes into eFa-next and when a version is released.
- Contributions are welcome as issues and pull requests; whatever is merged is under the license of the project, the
  AGPL-3.0-or-later.
- Security reports go the way `SECURITY.md` describes, support questions the way `SUPPORT.md` describes.
